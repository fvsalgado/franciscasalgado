    Updates with every gift. Cash given at the club and gifts through Fundação do Desporto are added by hand on the day.

      Companies

      Give with a tax benefit

      Companies operating in Portugal can give through Fundação do Desporto under the Portuguese sports patronage scheme, with a corporate tax benefit. Contact Pedro Salgado, Francisca's father: +351 938 885 386 · birdie@franciscasalgado.golf.

      Questions

      Who receives the money?

      Francisca's family. Every euro goes to the trip and, if there is any left, to her season.

      What if you pass €5,000?

      Anything above the target goes to the rest of her season, and we will say so on this page.

      Do I get a receipt?

      Yes. Stripe emails it straight after the payment.

      Can I give without paying online?

      Yes: at the club, on the day of the event, or by bank transfer. Write to birdie@franciscasalgado.golf.
