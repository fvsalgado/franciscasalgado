Privacy

How we handle your data

What is collected, what it is for, and what you can demand. In plain language, no small print.

Who handles the data

This site belongs to the golfer Francisca Salgado. For anything about personal data, or to exercise the rights below, use the contact form or a direct message on Instagram.

What is collected

Two sets only, and neither is collected unless the visitor causes it:

  • What you type into the contact form: name, email and, if you fill them in, organisation and message.
  • Usage measurement, if you allow it: pages viewed, time spent, device type, where the visit came from and a truncated IP address.

What it is for

Form data is used to answer your request and, if it goes ahead, to handle whatever was agreed. It is not used for newsletters and is not sold to anyone. Measurement is used to see which pages get read and to improve the site.

On what grounds

Handling form data rests on pre-contractual steps and on the legitimate interest of answering whoever writes. Measurement rests on consent alone, which you can give or withdraw whenever you like, from the “Cookies” button at the foot of any page.

Cookies

Until somebody consents, this site writes no cookie that is not essential to make it work. Your language and theme preference stay in your own browser and never leave it.

Third-party content

Some pages show content hosted elsewhere — Instagram posts, for instance. Those services may set their own cookies, under their own policies.

For how long

Contact requests are kept for as long as the relationship lasts and, after that, for the applicable legal period. Measurement data, where it exists, is deleted after fourteen months.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction or objection, and portability. You may withdraw consent at any time. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority at cnpd.pt.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The version in force is always the one on this page.
