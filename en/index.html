Amateur golfer · Portuguese national team · U18

Francisca Salgado

From Lisbon to the courses of Europe. At seventeen, five national titles — the latest the U18 — and four national runner-up finishes. She plays for the national team and has competed in eight countries.

Who she is

She took up golf at seven, at the Jamor, and won her first national title at ten. She is currently part of the Portuguese women's amateur national team and trains at the National Golf Training Centre, at the Jamor, and at the Academia de Lisboa, where she builds her preparation for elite competition.
