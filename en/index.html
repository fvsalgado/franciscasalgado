Who she is

National champion at ten, and again at seventeen

She won her first national championship in 2019, in the U10 category. She plays for Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos, and wears the shirt of the Portuguese women's amateur national team. In 2026 she added two more titles: the Amateur Pairs and the U18.
