Short biography

Francisca Salgado is an amateur golfer from Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos, and a member of the Portuguese women's amateur national team. She has four national titles: U10 in 2019, U12 in 2021 and, in 2026, the Amateur Pairs — alongside Rodrigo Constantino — and the U18. She won the GJG Algarve Juniors International in 2024, was runner-up in the FPG Cup in 2025 and third at the Memorial Celia Barquín, in Asturias. In 2026 she finished the English Girls' Open in 28th. She is supported by the Fundação do Desporto.
