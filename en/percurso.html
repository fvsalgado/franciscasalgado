Her story

National champion at ten, and again at seventeen

Amateur golfer from Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos. Portuguese women's amateur national team. Reigning national U18 champion.

She won her first national championship at ten. Seven years on she has four, plays the European calendar, and has not yet outgrown the U18 category.

She plays for Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos, and represents the Portuguese women's amateur national team. Four national titles in seven years — U10, U12, Amateur Pairs and U18 — and a season that has already taken her to Spain, France, England and Ireland. She is supported by the Fundação do Desporto.

What follows is the story year by year, from that first national podium to the seventeen years old she is now. Every event, with its rounds and its source, is on the results page.

    Questions

    The essentials, in six answers

    What you need to know without reading the whole page. Every answer is sourced elsewhere on this site.

    Who is Francisca Salgado?
    She is a Portuguese amateur golfer from Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos. At seventeen she has four national titles and plays for the Portuguese women's amateur national team, with which she plays the European calendar.
    How many national titles does she have?
    Four. U10 in 2019, U12 in 2021, and in 2026 both the Amateur Pairs — with Rodrigo Constantino — and the U18, which she won at Aroeira leading from start to finish.
    What club does she play for?
    Vale de Janelas, in Óbidos, on Portugal's west coast. She started at Paço do Lumiar, where she won her first national title in 2019.
    Where does she stand in the rankings?
    She is on the World Amateur Golf Ranking, the world ranking of amateur golf, and on the European Golf Rankings. Both positions are read from the official profiles the moment the page opens, and are on the results page.
    What are her best international results?
    Sixth, and the best Portuguese player, at the Belgian International U14 Championship in 2023; third at the Memorial Celia Barquín, in Asturias, in 2025; and 28th at the 2026 English Girls' Open, one of the strongest girls' U18 events in Europe, where she stood fifth at one point.
    How do you reach her about sponsorship or press?
    Through the form on the partnerships page or by direct message on Instagram, at @francisca_salgado_. Requests for high-resolution photographs go through the press page.
