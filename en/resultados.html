2025

9 events · 5 podiums · 1 win

The season that put her on the European map

She made her senior European team debut at sixteen. She was invited to play, as an amateur, the Super Bock Ladies Open at Vidago Palace — the first women's European professional event in Portugal in eight years. She won the Drive Tour at the Tejo, was runner-up in the FPG Cup at Santo Estêvão, and ended the year third in Asturias, at the Memorial Celia Barquín.

2024

12 events · 9 podiums · 6 wins

The year she won everything there was to win

She won the first two Aquapor Circuit tournaments and ended the season top of the women's “Gold” ranking. On the Drive Tour she won two of the four events she played and was runner-up in the other two. Over the summer she went top of the BPI national amateur ranking, and finished the year second, behind Inês Belchior. She was national U16 runner-up, at Tróia. And she closed the year winning the GJG Algarve Juniors International, in Castro Marim, ahead of the whole women's field and of the U18 category — with the only sub-par round of the week.
